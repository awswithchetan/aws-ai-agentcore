# ShopSmart Payment Methods

## Accepted Payment Methods

ShopSmart accepts a wide range of payment options to make checkout as convenient as possible.

## Credit and Debit Cards

- **Visa**
- **Mastercard**
- **American Express**
- **Discover**

All major credit and debit cards are accepted. Cards are charged at the time of order placement.

## Digital Wallets

- **PayPal** — Pay using your PayPal balance or linked bank account
- **Apple Pay** — Available on Safari and iOS devices
- **Google Pay** — Available on Chrome and Android devices
- **Shop Pay** — Save payment and shipping info for faster checkout

## Buy Now, Pay Later

- **Affirm** — Split your purchase into monthly installments (0%–30% APR, subject to approval)
- **Klarna** — Pay in 4 interest-free installments over 6 weeks
- **Afterpay** — 4 interest-free payments every 2 weeks

Minimum order value for Buy Now, Pay Later: $35.00

## ShopSmart Gift Cards

- Available in denominations of $25, $50, $100, and $200
- Can be purchased in-store or online
- Never expire
- Can be combined with other payment methods at checkout
- Not reloadable — purchase a new card when the balance runs out
- Lost or stolen gift cards cannot be replaced without the original receipt

## ShopSmart Store Credit

- Issued automatically for returns processed as store credit
- Applied automatically at checkout when logged into your account
- Valid for 12 months from the date of issue
- Can be combined with other payment methods

## Payment Security

- All transactions are encrypted using SSL/TLS technology
- ShopSmart is PCI-DSS compliant
- We never store your full card number
- Two-factor authentication available for account security

## Billing Information

- Your billing address must match the address on file with your bank
- Mismatched billing addresses may result in payment failure
- For international orders, ensure your card is enabled for international transactions

## Payment Issues

### Card Declined
Common reasons for card declines:
- Incorrect card number, expiry date, or CVV
- Billing address mismatch
- Insufficient funds
- Bank blocked the transaction (contact your bank)

### Payment Pending
- Payments may show as "pending" for up to 24 hours
- If the order is confirmed, the payment will be captured automatically
- If the order fails, any pending charge will be released within 3–5 business days

## Invoices and Receipts

- An email receipt is sent automatically after every successful order
- Itemised invoices can be downloaded from your account under Order History
- For business purchases requiring formal invoices, contact our business support team
