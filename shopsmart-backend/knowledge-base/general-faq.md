# ShopSmart General FAQ

## Orders

**How do I place an order?**
Browse products on the ShopSmart website or app, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You can check out as a guest or log into your account for a faster experience and order tracking.

**Can I modify or cancel my order?**
Orders can be modified or cancelled within **1 hour** of placement. After that, the order enters processing and cannot be changed. Contact support immediately if you need to make changes.

**What happens if an item in my order is out of stock?**
If an item becomes unavailable after you place your order, we will notify you by email and issue a full refund for that item. The rest of your order will ship as normal.

**How do I use a promo code or coupon?**
Enter your promo code in the "Promo Code" field at checkout before completing payment. Only one promo code can be applied per order. Promo codes cannot be applied after an order is placed.

**Can I place an order over the phone?**
Yes. Call 1-800-SHOPSMART during business hours and a support agent can assist with placing your order.

## Account

**How do I create an account?**
Click "Sign Up" on the ShopSmart website or app. You will need a valid email address and a password. Account creation is free.

**I forgot my password. How do I reset it?**
Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter your email address. You will receive a password reset link within a few minutes. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive.

**How do I update my shipping address or payment method?**
Log into your account, go to "Account Settings," and update your information under "Addresses" or "Payment Methods."

**Can I have multiple shipping addresses saved?**
Yes. You can save up to 10 shipping addresses in your account.

**How do I delete my account?**
Contact customer support to request account deletion. Note that deleting your account will remove all order history and any unused store credit.

## Products

**How do I know if an item is in stock?**
Product pages display current stock status. Items marked "In Stock" are available for immediate shipping. "Low Stock" means limited quantities remain. "Out of Stock" items cannot be ordered but you can sign up for a restock notification.

**Are ShopSmart products authentic?**
Yes. ShopSmart only sells authentic products sourced directly from brands or authorised distributors. We have a zero-tolerance policy for counterfeit goods.

**Do products come with a manufacturer warranty?**
Yes, products sold by ShopSmart include the standard manufacturer warranty. Warranty terms vary by product and brand. Contact the manufacturer directly for warranty claims.

**How do I find my size?**
Each clothing and footwear product page includes a size guide. If you are between sizes, we recommend sizing up. For specific fit questions, contact support.

## Pricing and Promotions

**Does ShopSmart offer price matching?**
Yes. If you find the same item at a lower price on a competitor's website within 7 days of your purchase, contact support with a link to the competitor's listing. We will match the price and refund the difference.

**How do ShopSmart sales work?**
Sale prices are applied automatically at checkout — no code required. Sale periods and discounts are announced on our website and email newsletter.

**Can I use multiple promotions together?**
In most cases, only one promotion can be applied per order. Some exclusions apply (e.g., sale items may not be eligible for additional discount codes). Terms are stated on each promotion.

## Loyalty Program — ShopSmart Rewards

**What is ShopSmart Rewards?**
ShopSmart Rewards is our free loyalty programme. Earn 1 point for every $1 spent. Points can be redeemed for store credit at a rate of 100 points = $1.

**How do I join?**
ShopSmart Rewards membership is included automatically with every ShopSmart account.

**When do points expire?**
Points expire after **12 months** of account inactivity (no purchases).

**Can I transfer my points to someone else?**
No. Points are non-transferable and tied to your account.

## Privacy and Security

**How does ShopSmart protect my personal data?**
ShopSmart uses industry-standard encryption for all data transmission and storage. We never sell your personal data to third parties. See our full Privacy Policy at shopsmart.com/privacy.

**Does ShopSmart share my information with third parties?**
We share data only with service providers necessary to fulfil your order (e.g., shipping carriers, payment processors). We do not sell your data.

**How do I opt out of marketing emails?**
Click "Unsubscribe" at the bottom of any ShopSmart marketing email, or update your communication preferences in Account Settings.
