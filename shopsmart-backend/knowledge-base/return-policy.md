# ShopSmart Return Policy

## Overview

ShopSmart wants you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you are not happy with an item, we make returns simple and hassle-free.

## Return Window

- Standard items: **30 days** from the date of delivery
- Electronics: **15 days** from the date of delivery
- Sale and clearance items: **14 days** from the date of delivery
- Gift purchases: **60 days** from the date of delivery (with gift receipt)

## Item Condition Requirements

To be eligible for a return, items must meet the following conditions:

- In original, unused condition
- With all original tags attached
- In original packaging where applicable
- Not washed, worn, or altered in any way
- Free from damage caused by the customer

## Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

- Perishable goods (food, flowers)
- Intimate apparel and swimwear (for hygiene reasons)
- Downloadable software and digital products
- Gift cards
- Hazardous materials
- Items marked as "Final Sale"

## How to Initiate a Return

1. Contact ShopSmart support or use the self-service return portal
2. Provide your order number and the item(s) you wish to return
3. Select your reason for return
4. Print the prepaid return label (emailed to you within minutes)
5. Pack the item securely and drop it off at any UPS location

## Return Shipping Costs

- **Defective or damaged items**: Free return shipping
- **Wrong item received**: Free return shipping
- **Change of mind / sizing issues**: $5.99 return shipping fee (deducted from refund)

## Refund Processing

- Refunds are issued to the original payment method
- Processing time: **3–5 business days** after the returned item is received and inspected
- You will receive an email confirmation when your refund is processed
- Credit card refunds may take an additional 2–3 business days to appear on your statement

## Exchanges

ShopSmart does not process direct exchanges. To get a different size or color:

1. Return the original item following the standard return process
2. Place a new order for the desired item

This ensures you get the fastest possible delivery on the replacement item.

## Damaged or Defective Items

If you received a damaged or defective item:

- Contact support within **48 hours** of delivery
- Provide photos of the damage
- We will arrange a free return and send a replacement or issue a full refund — your choice

## Return Status

You can check your return status by:

- Logging into your ShopSmart account and viewing order history
- Contacting customer support with your order number
- Using the tracking number on your return label
