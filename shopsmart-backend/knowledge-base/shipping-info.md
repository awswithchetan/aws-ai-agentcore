# ShopSmart Shipping Information

## Domestic Shipping Options (United States)

### Standard Shipping
- **Delivery time**: 5–7 business days
- **Cost**: $4.99 per order
- **Free on orders over**: $50.00
- **Carrier**: UPS, USPS (based on destination)

### Express Shipping
- **Delivery time**: 2–3 business days
- **Cost**: $9.99 per order
- **Available for**: All items except oversized/heavy items
- **Carrier**: UPS

### Overnight Shipping
- **Delivery time**: Next business day (order by 2 PM EST)
- **Cost**: $19.99 per order
- **Available for**: In-stock items only, select zip codes
- **Carrier**: UPS Overnight

### Same-Day Delivery
- **Delivery time**: Within 4 hours of order placement
- **Cost**: $14.99 per order
- **Available in**: New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Houston, Phoenix, Philadelphia, San Antonio, San Diego, Dallas, San Jose
- **Order cutoff**: 12 PM local time
- **Carrier**: Local courier partners

## International Shipping

ShopSmart ships to **30 countries** worldwide.

### Available Regions
- Canada
- United Kingdom
- European Union (select countries)
- Australia and New Zealand
- Japan and South Korea
- Select countries in Latin America and Southeast Asia

### International Shipping Rates
- **Canada**: $12.99 (7–10 business days)
- **United Kingdom / EU**: $19.99 (10–14 business days)
- **Australia / New Zealand**: $24.99 (12–18 business days)
- **Japan / South Korea**: $22.99 (10–14 business days)
- **Other countries**: Rates calculated at checkout

### International Customs and Duties
- Customers are responsible for any customs duties, taxes, or import fees
- These are not included in ShopSmart's shipping charges
- ShopSmart is not responsible for delays caused by customs processing

## Order Processing Time

- Orders placed before **2 PM EST on business days** are processed the same day
- Orders placed after 2 PM EST or on weekends are processed the next business day
- During peak seasons (Black Friday, Cyber Monday, holidays), processing may take 1–2 additional business days

## Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive:
- A shipping confirmation email with your tracking number
- A link to track your package in real time

You can also track your order by:
- Logging into your ShopSmart account
- Contacting customer support with your order number

## Shipping Restrictions

The following items have shipping restrictions:
- **Oversized items** (furniture, large appliances): Standard and Express only, no overnight or same-day
- **Hazardous materials**: Ground shipping only, no air shipping
- **Alcohol**: Available only in select states, adult signature required on delivery

## Missed Delivery and Undeliverable Packages

- If a delivery attempt is missed, UPS/USPS will leave a notice and attempt redelivery
- After 3 failed delivery attempts, the package is held at the local facility for 5 business days
- Undeliverable packages are returned to ShopSmart and a full refund is issued (minus original shipping cost)
- Packages returned due to incorrect address provided by the customer may incur a reshipping fee

## Lost or Stolen Packages

- If your tracking shows "delivered" but you have not received the package, contact us within **7 days**
- ShopSmart will file a claim with the carrier on your behalf
- Resolution time: 5–10 business days
- If the claim is approved, we will reship the order or issue a full refund
