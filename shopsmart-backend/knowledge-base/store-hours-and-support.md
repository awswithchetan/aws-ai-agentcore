# ShopSmart Customer Support Hours and Contact

## AI Support — Aria

Aria, ShopSmart's AI customer support agent, is available **24 hours a day, 7 days a week** including holidays.

Aria can help with:
- Order status and tracking
- Return requests and refund status
- Product information and availability
- Shipping questions
- Payment and billing queries
- General FAQ

## Live Customer Support

### Phone Support
- **Hours**: Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM EST
- **Phone**: 1-800-SHOPSMART (1-800-746-7762)
- **Average wait time**: Under 5 minutes during business hours

### Live Chat
- **Hours**: Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM EST
- **Saturday**: 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM EST
- **Sunday**: Closed
- Available via the ShopSmart website and mobile app

### Email Support
- **Email**: support@shopsmart.com
- **Available**: 24/7
- **Response time**: Within 4 hours during business hours, within 12 hours on weekends

### Social Media Support
- **Twitter/X**: @ShopSmartHelp
- **Facebook**: facebook.com/ShopSmartSupport
- **Response time**: Within 2 hours during business hours

## Business and Bulk Orders

For business accounts, bulk purchasing, or corporate orders:
- **Email**: business@shopsmart.com
- **Hours**: Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM EST
- Dedicated account managers available for orders over $5,000

## Accessibility Support

ShopSmart is committed to accessible customer service:
- **TTY/TDD**: 1-800-746-7763
- Large print and audio format materials available on request
- Website compliant with WCAG 2.1 AA standards

## Holiday Hours

Customer support hours may be reduced on the following holidays:
- New Year's Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day (July 4th)
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day
- Christmas Day

Aria remains available 24/7 on all holidays.

## Escalation

If your issue is not resolved by Aria or the first support agent:
- Request to speak with a supervisor during live chat or phone support
- Email escalations@shopsmart.com for unresolved complaints
- We aim to resolve all escalated issues within **2 business days**
